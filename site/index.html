      What is real, what is sample or concept

      Real
        Sample or concept
        Macros are estimates from USDA reference values. Customer names, reviews, B2B accounts and order volumes in the team views are samples. Concept products (plans, tiers, bundles) are priced from real portion prices and marked “Concept”. Revenue figures are estimates built on public benchmarks, not on J&W’s own sales.

        What would sharpen the estimates

        • 12 months of Shopify orders
        • Store vs online vs wholesale split
        • Repeat-customer rate
        • Plant capacity and cook schedule
        • Margins by category
        • Delivery cost per route
