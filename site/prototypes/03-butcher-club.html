Questions

Questions

How do points work?

Each cut has a point price, about $5 of our shelf price per point. Your box size sets your points. Extra points are $5 each. Up to 5 unused points roll to your next box.

When can I change my box?

Until Sunday 11:59 pm before your route day. After that we start cutting and packing.

Can I skip or cancel?

Yes. Skip, swap, change how often, pause or cancel online. No contract, no fee.

How does delivery work?

On your normal J&W route day, frozen and vacuum-sealed. Members don't pay the route fee. We text your window the day before.

Where's the meat from?

Our chicken is Ontario-raised, air-chilled and grain-fed, with no hormones or antibiotics. Our beef is graded AAA, with Wagyu and Canadian Prime available. Lamb chops and whole lamb are Ontario-raised.

Can you ship outside Ontario?

No. We're an Ontario-inspected plant, so our meat stays in Ontario. Our Friday long-haul route reaches Ottawa, Kingston, London and Windsor.

What if something arrives thawed?

Text us a photo the same day and we'll replace it in your next box.
