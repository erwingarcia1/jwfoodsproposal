      Questions buyers ask

      Can people outside Ontario get a gift?

      Not yet. Our plant is Ontario-inspected, so we can only sell meat within Ontario. We flag those addresses before you pay.

      Do certificates expire?

      Prepaid Pick-a-Box certificates don't expire.

      Is a gift a taxable benefit?

      The CRA allows up to $500 a year in non-cash gifts per employee, tax included. We'll send a gift record for your payroll team. Please check with your accountant.

      What if someone doesn't eat beef or pork?

      A certificate lets them pick a Meal Butler box of chicken, salmon and egg bites. For allergies, call us first.

      How cold does it stay?

      Boxes leave frozen in insulated liners with ice packs. For office drops, please have someone receive them and a freezer or cooler ready.

      Can we pay by invoice?

      Yes, Net 30 for approved businesses, with a 50% deposit on a first order.

      Is there a minimum?

      No. Volume pricing starts at 10 gifts.

      Can we pick up?

      Yes, free at 2201 Brimley Rd, Mon-Fri 9-6, Sat 10-4.
