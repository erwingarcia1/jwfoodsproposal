# Play 3: J&W Butcher Club

Modelled on **truLOCAL** (https://trulocal.ca, Canada, founded Kitchener ON 2016). Ideas only, never their copy.
Prepared for Minastech (Erwin Garcia) · October 16, 2026 · All CAD. Every figure is an ESTIMATE unless it is a J&W published price or a cited source.

## 1. The play in one paragraph

A raw-meat membership for GTA households. Members pick a box size (Starter, Family or Freezer Fill), choose how often (every 2, 4, 6 or 8 weeks) and spend a points budget on real J&W cuts. The box is cut, vacuum-sealed and frozen at 2201 Brimley Rd and rides **free** on the member's normal J&W route day. Every box includes one **free-for-life item**, by default a Meal Butler cooked chicken 4 x 4 oz pack, which puts J&W's higher-margin cooked line in front of raw-meat buyers. The top of the range is **beef and lamb shares** (1/16, 1/8, 1/4 beef, whole and half Ontario lamb) with a cut sheet, planning weight and deposit. Launch is timed for Wild Fork's Ontario exit: its Whitby, Oakville, Ancaster and Newmarket stores close by Dec 14, 2026, and all four towns are on J&W routes.

**Why it makes money:** it turns occasional freezer stock-ups into a recurring box on routes J&W already drives, at butcher-counter prices well below Toronto's premium shops, and it uses a free cooked pack to move raw buyers into Meal Butler.

### 4.3 Math notes

- **Churn.** truLOCAL's CLTV (~$2,000) ÷ AOV (~$240) implies about 8 lifetime orders. Our 0.95 boxes a month ÷ 11% churn = 8.6 lifetime boxes. That matches, and it sits between replenishment churn (5–8%) and meal-kit churn (8–15%) in market-benchmarks.md.
- **Boxes per month.** Cadence mix 15% every 2 weeks, 60% every 4, 15% every 6, 10% every 8 gives 1.14 scheduled boxes a month; about 12–15% skips brings it to 0.95.
- **AOV.** Tier mix 35% Starter / 45% Family / 20% Freezer Fill = $220, plus $15 of extra points and Meal Butler add-ons = $235. truLOCAL's ~$240 AOV supports it.
- **Margin.** 30% blended, per model-assumptions.md (raw boxes 25–35%). Build-up: if J&W's portioned raw shelf price carries about 35%, the free item (~4.5 pts), tote and liner (~1.4 pts) and Freezer Fill bonus points (~0.6 pts) bring the box to about 28.5%; $15 of add-ons at 45% lifts the blend to about 30%. If J&W's real raw margin is lower, cut the bonus points and the sirloin-level free options first.
- **Incrementality 0.75** (low end of 0.75–0.85) because this play converts some of J&W's existing raw buyers.
- **Cannibalisation cost** not covered by the factor: members who would have ordered anyway stop paying the route fee and now get a free item. About 25% of boxes x ($20 fee + ~$10 item) = **~$12.6K in year 1, ~$24.5K in year 2**. Counted in contribution below.
- **No separate Wild Fork line.** Early screening added "150 households x $150 x 10 months = $225K" for Wild Fork switchers. We do not add it; those households are inside the 415 joins, so there is no double count.
- **Sanity check.** Year 1 gross Club revenue is ~17% of J&W's ~$2.5M mid-case revenue; new revenue is +13% in year 1 and +24% at run-rate. Year 2 is about 63 boxes a week and about 1,250 lb a week of extra frozen raw volume.
- **Contribution (ESTIMATE).** Year 1: gross profit $322K x 30% = $96.6K, less operating costs ~$60.4K, setup $11K and cannibalisation ~$12.6K = **about +$12.6K**. Year 2: $612K x 30% = $183.6K, less ~$76.8K costs and ~$24.5K cannibalisation = **about +$82K a year**. This is a low-margin, steady play. Its value is recurring volume and the cooked-line cross-sell, not big profit per box.

## 8. Assumptions

1. Soft launch Tue Dec 1, 2026; November is build and waitlist (no revenue).
2. 415 joins in year 1 (55 in December, 30–40 a month after) and 36 a month in year 2.
3. 11% monthly churn; 0.95 boxes per active member per month.
4. $235 average order: $220 box (35/45/20 tier mix) + $15 add-ons.
5. 30% blended gross margin; 0.75 incrementality.
6. A point = about $5 of J&W shelf price; J&W's raw shelf prices hold through year 1.
7. Route stop cost $10 (model range $8–14); 70% of Club boxes are new stops.
8. Share planning weights (quarter ~100–120 lb take-home) are estimates until J&W weighs a test quarter.
9. Shares year 1: 30 x 1/16, 15 x 1/8, 8 x 1/4, 8 whole and 6 half lamb; year 2 is 1.4x.
10. Wild Fork switchers sit inside the join numbers, not in a separate line.
11. J&W's vans and route times keep boxes frozen; long-haul boxes get an insulated tote with gel or dry ice.
12. Shopify Subscriptions (or Recharge/Loop) plus a bundle app can run a points builder. A custom builder is a fallback.

## 9. Risks

1. **Thin margin.** Raw is about 30%. The free item and free delivery can push a Starter box under 25% if J&W's real shelf margin is below 35%. Confirm SKU costs before launch.
2. **Cannibalisation.** Existing raw buyers join and stop paying route fees. Track each member's spend before joining; the $139 minimum box protects order size.
3. **Churn above 11%.** Meal-kit churn runs 8–15%. Mitigate with skip and pause, slower cadences, the Thursday reminder and payment retries (failed payments cause about half of subscription churn).
4. **Cold chain and capacity.** Year 2 adds ~1,250 lb a week of frozen volume. Check van freezer space, Brimley freezer space and route times, especially Friday long-haul.
5. **Share weights.** Unstated weights invite disputes. Weigh a test quarter and sell with a guaranteed minimum take-home weight.
6. **Wild Fork name.** Trademark and ad-platform rules limit use in ad text. Use factual town and date messaging; legal review first.
7. **Ontario-only.** J&W is provincially inspected, so Club boxes cannot leave Ontario. The Friday long-haul route covers Ottawa, Kingston, London and Windsor.
8. **Meat cost inflation.** truLOCAL's parent cited higher meat COGS in Q1 2026. Review point values quarterly.
9. **App fit.** Points-budget bundles may need a paid bundle app or custom code on Shopify.
10. **Overlap.** Keep the Club raw-only so it does not cannibalise Play 1 (cooked plans) or Play 5 (gifting).

## 10. Prototype spec (one self-contained page: `site/prototypes/03-butcher-club.html`)

### 10.1 Concept

A clickable, mobile-first Club page in J&W's voice. It shows truLOCAL's best ideas (tier picker, points-budget builder, free-for-life item, comparison table, self-serve membership) done the J&W way: real J&W cuts and prices, $/lb on every card, cut-to-thickness steaks, route-day delivery, Meal Butler cooked chicken as the free item, and beef shares with cut sheets. The JW team view shows the business underneath: a modelled week in April 2027 with KPIs, the Monday pick list, route impact, agent drafts to approve, a retention queue, the share board and a monthly P&L.

**Wow moment:** the box fills as you shop. A top-down "freezer box" fills with a tile for each pack (photo + name, sized by weight). A sky-blue points meter fills, and a live line updates ("21.3 lb · about 68 servings · $11.22/lb"). The slate-purple free-for-life tile is pinned in a corner from the start. When points hit the budget, the lid closes with a navy laurel seal reading "Packed at 2201 Brimley Rd" and the button turns into "Join with this box".
