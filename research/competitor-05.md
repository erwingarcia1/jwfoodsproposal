# Competitor #5: Omaha Steaks (Business Gifts), the global B2B gifting benchmark

Prepared for Minastech (Erwin Garcia) for the J&W Foods / J&W Meal Butler proposal. Research date: **October 16, 2026.**
Method: each page was opened in a real browser, because omahasteaks.com returns **HTTP 403 (Akamai bot block) to WebFetch and curl**. The pages themselves are live. business.omahasteaks.com pages were read with both WebFetch and the browser. The web-search budget for this run was used up, so no third-party news or revenue sources were added. Every figure below comes from Omaha Steaks' own pages, J&W's catalog, or the Canadian government.

---

## 4. Flows worth borrowing, step by step

**A. Landing-page lead capture (pops up on arrival)** ([business-gifts](https://www.omahasteaks.com/business-gifts))
1. A "Business Gift Offers" modal opens, promising "exclusive volume discounts, branded gift selections and easy ordering from our Business Gift Experts".
2. Fields: First, Last, Business Name, Work Email, Business Phone, Comments.
3. Two pill-button questions qualify the lead in one tap: gift count (<10 … 500+) and spend per gift (<$100 … >$250).
4. A Business Gift Expert follows up. Sales gets a pre-sorted lead without the buyer typing a budget.

**B. Business-gifts landing structure** (a useful wireframe)
1. Hero: "Corporate Gifts Made Easy" with a single "Get Volume Pricing" CTA.
2. Proof band of four big numbers (100+ yrs, 5,000+, 350k+, 700k+).
3. Three program cards: Employee Gifts, Client Gifts, Marketing Promotions.
4. "Built around your program": branding, curation, choice, large programs.
5. Survey stats: 89% and 68%.
6. Business Rewards teaser, then a free Gift Guide download.
7. Case study in Challenge, Solution, Result format with a quote.
8. Insights articles, then "Get Expert Help" (volume, branding, flexible payment, support).
9. Four shoppable boxes, then resource cards (Large, How to Order, Certificates, Holiday).
10. Sticky action row: Call, Order Form, Quote, Gift Guide.

**C. Choice Gift Certificate, six steps** ([certificates](https://business.omahasteaks.com/resources/corporate-gift-certificates))
1. **Choose:** the certificate level, quantity, timing and payment structure.
2. **Customize:** logo, gift message, recipient name, campaign title or occasion wording. Logos are available only "for qualifying custom programs".
3. **Distribute:** mail to each recipient, ship in bulk to the office, or hand out at events. The quote form offers Printed (Bulk), Printed (Mailed), E-Certificate or Not Sure.
4. **Redeem:** online, by phone or by mail using the unique code.
5. **Choose:** the recipient picks from the curated collections at that level.
6. **Enjoy:** the gift is delivered, with standard shipping included.
- **Pay-on-redemption:** qualifying large programs pay only for certificates actually used.
- Use-case tiles: employee recognition, client appreciation, sales incentives/SPIFs, **safety awards**, loyalty and promotions (casino and dealer programs), events and golf outings.

**D. Multi-recipient ordering, four steps** ([how-to-order](https://business.omahasteaks.com/resources/how-to-order-corporate-gifts); [Large Orders](https://www.omahasteaks.com/info/Large-Gift-Orders))
1. Describe the program: audience, count, budget, occasion and timing.
2. Choose gifts, or have an expert curate a custom box.
3. Fill in **one Business Gift Order Form**, an **Excel .xlsx** file hosted on Pardot. It holds names, addresses, gift choice and a message for each recipient. Email it to largeorders@omahasteaks.com, fax it, or call 1-800-858-5335 (Mon–Fri 7am–10pm, Sat 8–7, Sun 9–5). Card details are taken by phone, never by email.
4. A dedicated expert coordinates shipments. The buyer pays with **a single invoice or one card charge**.
- With no recipient addresses, the buyer switches to Choice certificates and recipients enter their own address when they redeem.
- Repeat-program case study: the client "sent its updated list to its Account Manager" the following year. Year-over-year retention is the business model.

**E. Holiday planning calendar** ([holiday business gifts](https://business.omahasteaks.com/resources/holiday-business-gifts))
- July: set goals, audience and budget. August: review options. September: pick gifts and gather recipients. October: finalise branding and quantities. November: schedule early deliveries. December: use certificates for late needs.
- J&W should be at the October step right now.

**F. "Customize Your Box" points builder** ([builder](https://www.omahasteaks.com/servlet/OnlineShopping?Dsp=8500&sku=81977))
1. Start from a preset gift such as the Steakhouse Grill Gift at $148.
2. Each item carries a point value: chicken breasts 1 pt, NY strips 2–6 pts, Private Reserve filets 12 pts. The box needs **at least 5 points with no maximum**.
3. Category tabs: Steaks / Burgers & Franks / Chicken & Pork / Seafood / Meals, Sides & Sweets.
4. A sticky footer shows "Reset Box · 5 Points Used · $148 · Continue →", under a two-step progress bar (Home, then Customize).
- This maps directly onto a J&W Meal Butler week or a J&W Choice certificate, where points equal the certificate value.

**G. AI and service layer**
- A right-edge "Chat" tab runs a **Simple AI (usesimple.ai) assistant with voice capability**, welcoming visitors with "How can we help you today?". The iframe was observed on the page.
- A live phone line runs 24/7 (1-800-960-8400). The business desk is 1-800-228-2480.

## 6. Features to borrow (ranked for J&W's Q4 2026)

1. **J&W Choice e-certificate:** one code per recipient, redeemable for a box, a Meal Butler week or a Butcher Club month, with delivery booked on J&W's own route day. This solves the perishable-delivery and unknown-address problems. Under CRA rules it stays "non-cash" only if it can be used at a single retailer and cannot be turned into cash. The [CRA page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/payroll/benefits-allowances/gifts-awards-social-events/gifts-awards-long-service-awards.html), modified 2026-07-27, confirms the $500/yr non-cash limit including taxes and these card conditions.
2. **Pay-on-redemption** for larger programs: a strong close, and the cost to J&W is low because unredeemed codes are never produced.
3. **Two-tap lead qualifier** (gift count, then spend per gift) on a business landing page, with an AI or concierge follow-up.
4. **Points-based "Customize Your Box"** builder with a sticky footer showing points, price and Continue.
5. **Annual-spend rewards tiers.** Free logo placement at C$5K+, a personal-use certificate at C$5K+, and more at C$20K+, with Canadian thresholds to be set by J&W. This locks in repeat programs.
6. **Recipient-list ordering:** one spreadsheet, one invoice. J&W can go a step further with an in-browser CSV upload and a live preview, where Omaha still emails an Excel file.
7. **Holiday planning calendar plus "Plan your holiday program" CTA**, and year-round occasions (safety milestones, realtor closings, client thank-yous, Greek Easter).
8. **Proof band and case study** (Challenge, Solution, Result) once J&W has its first 3–5 corporate clients.
9. **Stores as fulfilment hubs.** Omaha cut delivery from 6+ days to 2 by shipping from stores. J&W's Brimley Rd plant already sits inside its delivery radius, so the claim is "GTA delivery on your route day, or pickup today".

## 7. What J&W already does better

- **It serves the GTA at all.** Omaha does not ship to Canada ([Shipping](https://www.omahasteaks.com/info/Shipping-Information)). J&W already runs weekly refrigerated routes across the GTA, Durham, Hamilton/Halton and York, plus long-haul to Ottawa, London, Windsor and others, and free pickup in Scarborough (jw-facts.md).
- **Local, inspected and traceable.** J&W has run an Ontario-inspected meat plant since 2008 and can cut to order in Scarborough. Its chicken is air-chilled, grain-fed and Ontario-raised with no hormones or antibiotics (jw-facts.md). Omaha's local story is about Nebraska.
- **Butcher heritage of a comparable kind:** a family butcher-grocer in downtown Toronto from the early 60s, Advance Food Products from 1973, and J&W from 1995. The scale differs, but it is the same "family butcher" story, and in Toronto it is local.
- **Cooked, portioned, healthy meals à la carte:** about 292 Meal Butler items, all "salt and sugar free, with no added oils", in 4–8 oz portions (jw-facts.md). Omaha's prepared foods are indulgent, such as scalloped potatoes, tartlets, Chicken Kiev and nuggets. A J&W corporate gift can be a **wellness gift**, such as "a week of clean meals for your team". Omaha cannot offer that.
- **Greek and Mediterranean identity:** A Taste of Greece Box at $130 and about 154 Mediterranean SKUs (jw-catalog.json). This gives GTA's Greek-Canadian businesses and Greek Easter gifting a reason to choose J&W.
- **Price per gift in CAD:** Gentleman's Box $129.99, Taste of Greece $130, Long Weekend $199, Girls Night $89.99, BBQ Dad Pack $84.99 (jw-catalog.json). These sit in the same $85–$200 range as Omaha's USD $81–$212 boxes. **But J&W delivers on its routes for a flat $20–$35 with a $100 minimum, and pickup is free** (jw-facts.md), while Omaha charges US$24.99 per address under $149.
- **Wholesale and whole-animal depth:** AAA/Prime/Wagyu, cases, quarter cow $1,200 and whole lamb. These support a premium "J&W Reserve" tier and office-catering add-ons.

**Gaps J&W must close before launching** (from the jw-catalog.json audit):
- No corporate or B2B page.
- Gift cards are e-cards only, at $10/$25/$50/$100 CAD, with no branding, bulk purchase or certificate option.
- The **"A Taste of Greece Box Special" description contains pasted voice-dictation text** ("Do you know what I know who it is so yeah don't worry…"). It must be fixed before the box is sold to companies.
- No reviews or ratings, while Omaha shows 686 reviews on a single gift.

## 8. Corrections to the pick's write-up

- The [business-gifts page](https://www.omahasteaks.com/business-gifts) **is live.** The 403 comes from Akamai bot protection blocking WebFetch and curl. The page loaded normally in a browser on Oct 16, 2026, and the 5,000+, 350k+ and 700k+ figures appear on it, labelled "Omaha Steaks internal data in 2025".
- "Volume pricing at bands from under 10 to 500+ recipients" overstates the page. Those bands are **quantity buckets on the lead form**. No volume-discount tiers or percentages are published; the wording is only "may be available for qualifying orders".
- "Certificates carry a company logo": logos are offered only "for qualifying custom programs". *Free* logo placement is a Business Rewards **Premier** benefit, which requires US$5,000+ in annual spend.
- Omaha's B2B flow is **not self-serve.** It runs on a lead form, an expert or account manager, and an emailed Excel order form. A self-serve CSV-upload studio would put J&W *ahead* of Omaha on ease of use.
- Omaha **does not ship to Canada.** It is a benchmark for how to run a business-gifting program, not a GTA competitor for the same buyers.
- Choice certificate price levels are not published. The rewards page implies a Platinum certificate at about $200 and a Diamond certificate at about $250.

## 9. Revenue note for the "Corporate Gift Studio" play

- These are the orchestrator's estimates, and they stay labelled as ESTIMATES.
  - Conservative case: 60 companies × 25 gifts × $130 ≈ $195K.
  - Upside case: about 115 firms × 60 boxes × $120 ≈ $830K (market-benchmarks.md §11; competitors-gta-meat-grocery.md).
- Omaha's own buckets support pricing the J&W tiers at about **C$85 / C$130 / C$200 / C$350.** Its lead form centres on $100–$250 per gift, and its 2026 holiday guide runs $100–$180.
- **Pay-on-redemption and rewards tiers** are the two Omaha tools most likely to turn first-year buyers into repeat annual programs. Its case study describes a repeat program where the client simply sent an updated list.
