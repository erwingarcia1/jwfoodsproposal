# Play 2: J&W Pro Partner Portal

Prepared for Minastech (Erwin Garcia) for J&W Foods / J&W Meal Butler · October 16, 2026
All money in CAD. Every figure is an ESTIMATE unless it is a J&W published price (jwfoods.com, Oct 16 2026) or carries a source. Shared figures come from `model-assumptions.md`.

**One-liner (hub card):** A wholesale portal where GTA gyms, trainers, clinics, supplement shops and teams apply in two minutes, get tiered case pricing on Meal Butler meals and butcher cases, and receive standing weekly drops on J&W's own routes.

**Why it makes money:** Gyms, trainers and clinics already send people to J&W by word of mouth. A real partner program turns that into standing weekly case orders that fill the plant and make the routes denser, with almost all of the volume new to J&W.

**Focus:** B2B wholesale portal for gyms, clinics, trainers and shops.

## 2. The mechanism: what J&W changes

J&W already sells "semi-wholesale" online: 4 kg chicken cases ($92-$100), a 10 lb salmon case ($175), a 25-pack of chicken burgers ($35.50) and a "BUY IN BULK & SAVE" banner. Trainers recommend J&W to clients ("I recommend all of my clients", Google review via Chamber of Commerce). What's missing is a front door for businesses. This play adds five things:

1. **A partner page and a 2-minute application** for six groups: gyms and CrossFit boxes, personal trainers and coaches, physio/dietitian/weight-loss clinics, supplement and health shops, boxing/MMA/fight gyms, and teams and academies (team nutrition for travel days and camps). An AI pre-check maps the address to a route day, suggests a tier and a starter order, and a person (Lucas or the account lead) approves within one business day.
2. **Tiered partner pricing (Concept)**, set by the average weekly order over four weeks, as a discount off J&W's own web price:

   | Tier | Average weekly order | Meal Butler cooked food | Butcher cases | Extras |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | Partner | $150+ | 15% off | 5% off | Free drop on route day, member code, net-15 after 4 paid orders |
   | Pro | $300+ | 20% off | 8% off | + loaned reach-in freezer, co-branded labels |
   | Elite | $600+ | 25% off | 10% off | + first pick of production slots, quarterly menu tasting |

   Founding offer: the first 10 accounts get Pro pricing for 12 months. Partners agree to sell at J&W's web price or higher, never lower, so jwfoods.com is never undercut.
3. **Concept case packs built from real portion prices** (see §3). Example: Jerk Chicken, Green Beans & Sweet Potato (6 oz) is $14.99 on jwfoods.com. A 12-pack is $179.88 at web price and $143.90 at Pro. The gym sells each meal at $14.99 and keeps $3.00.
4. **Standing weekly orders on the existing routes.** Minimum 12 cooked meals or one butcher case per order. Order by Sunday, delivered on the partner's route day (GTA 1 Tue, GTA 2 Wed, GTA 3 Thu, GTA 4 Fri) or picked up free at Brimley Rd. Standing orders of $150+ drop free (J&W credits the $20-$27 route fee). Card for the first four orders, then net-15 by invoice.
5. **Partner tools:** a free tasting tray of 24 portions on the first route day; a loaned, branded reach-in freezer with a temperature sensor once an account averages $300+ a week for eight weeks; "Fuelled by J&W at [Partner]" co-branded labels that keep J&W's plant details, ingredients and allergens; and a member code (10% off a member's first jwfoods.com order, 5% of members' online spend back to the partner as J&W credit for 12 months). Member-code DTC sales are tracked but **not counted** in this play's revenue.

**Build:** on J&W's existing Shopify store. Either a wholesale-pricing app on the current plan (ESTIMATE ~$100/month) or Shopify's native B2B features (company accounts, catalogues, payment terms), which have been tied to Shopify Plus; confirm current plan rules. Recommendation: start with the app and move to Plus only if partner sales pass ~$40K a month.

**Timeline:**
- **Nov 2026:** price list, partner agreement, application form, wholesale app, label templates and nutrition analysis started. Recruit 5 founding partners from existing trainer and gym customers and Instagram.
- **Dec 2026:** first drops (startMonth 2). Tasting trays go out before the January gym rush.
- **Jan to Apr 2027:** "New year, stocked freezer" outreach along the GTA 1-4 corridors. First loaned freezers. Net-15 opens for accounts with 4 paid orders.
- **Aug 2027:** CanFitPro Toronto booth (J&W exhibited in 2014; confirm 2027 dates).
- **Sep to Oct 2027:** varsity and academy season starts. Target 34 active accounts.

**Kept distinct from the other six plays:** offices and employer-funded meals are Play 4 (J&W at Work). Team and school fundraisers are Play 7 (Team Fuel Fundraiser); here a team buys food for its own athletes. Consumer subscriptions are Play 1 and the AI coach is Play 6. Restaurants and J&W's existing informal trade accounts are not counted.

**Mid:** 224 account-months x 4.33 weeks = 970 account-weeks x $260 average weekly order (new accounts start near $180; freezer accounts reach $400+) = $252,200 gross x 0.85 incremental = **$214,400 net new revenue**. Gross profit at 22% = **$47,200**.

**Low:** ramp to 20 accounts by Oct (124 account-months) x 4.33 = 537 account-weeks x $220 = $118,140 x 0.80 = **$94,500**.

**High:** ramp to 50 accounts by Oct (330 account-months) x 4.33 = 1,429 account-weeks x $300 = $428,700 x 0.85 = **$364,400**.

### Run-rate (year 2, Nov 2027 to Oct 2028)

Accounts grow from 34 to about 52: **44 average active x $330/week x 50 weeks = $726,000 gross x 0.85 = $617,100 net new revenue** (gross profit at 22% = $135,800). Range: low 30 x $280 x 50 x 0.80 = $336,000; high 55 x $380 x 50 x 0.85 = $888,250.

### Notes on the math

- **Margin, 22% blended (model-assumptions.md).** At Pro, cooked food keeps about 31% margin after the 20% discount (from 45%), and cases about 21% after 8% off (from ~27%). A 65/35 cooked/case mix gives about 27-28% before Elite discounts, credits, shrink and mix risk. We plan on the model's 22%. Freezers, van time and staff are counted separately in costs, not inside the margin.
- **Incrementality, 0.85.** Most volume is new (a gym freezer sells to people who would not have placed a $100 route order). Some trainers and gym owners already buy cases at retail and will move to partner pricing. The Application Checker flags existing customers so the overlap stays visible.
- **Funnel to 40 gross wins in year 1:** ~2,000 businesses contacted along the route corridors x ~4% reply (Apollo SMB test: 4.2%) = ~80, plus ~60 inbound from Instagram (30K), the store and trainer referrals = ~140 conversations, ~80 tasting trays, ~50% tasting-to-account = ~40 wins, less ~6 churned = 34 active.
- **Pool:** ~2,000 GTA gyms and clubs (ESTIMATE from IBISWorld's 4,195 Ontario establishments), 59 Ontario CrossFit affiliates, ~31,460 fitness instructors and trainers in the Toronto region (Job Bank), ~8-12K independent trainers (ESTIMATE). 52 accounts is a small slice of this.
- **Sanity check vs J&W:** year-2 run-rate is ~25% of the $2.5M mid-case revenue. It needs roughly 940 extra cooked meals and ~50 butcher cases a week. Plant capacity is the first thing to confirm with J&W (market-benchmarks.md §12).
- **JW's own costs and contribution.** Year 1: ~$26,000 setup + ~$3,400/month Dec-Oct + ~$1,600 in Nov = ~$65,000, against $47,200 gross profit, so about **-$18,000 in year 1** (mostly freezers). Year 2: ~$5,300/month (~$64,000) + 8 more freezers ($14,400) = ~$78,000, against $135,800 gross profit, so about **+$58,000**. That is before the plant's fixed-cost absorption and before member-code DTC sales. This is a volume and utilisation play, not a high-margin one.
- **Upside not counted:** member-code DTC sales. Example: 44 partners x 3 member orders a month x $130 x 12 = ~$206,000 a year at 45% margin. Excluded here to avoid double counting with Plays 1 and 6.
- **Levers to improve contribution:** push the cooked share of the mix to 75% (+2 points of margin), buy refurbished freezers, and keep Elite (25% off) for accounts over $600 a week.

## 10. Risks and how to handle them

1. **Thin margin.** At 22%, a few off-list deals wipe out the profit. Only tier prices in the portal; any exception needs the owner's approval.
2. **Cannibalisation.** Existing case-buying trainers may switch to partner pricing. The Partner tier needs a business and $150+ a week. The Application Checker flags existing customers.
3. **Resale labelling.** Food resold by a third party needs full prepackaged labels (ingredients, allergens, net weight, best-before, dealer name and address, and a Nutrition Facts table unless an exemption applies; bilingual rules apply). J&W publishes no nutrition panels today. Budget the analysis and confirm the rules with a labelling consultant before the first freezer goes out.
4. **Cold chain in someone else's freezer.** Require −18°C or colder, fit a Wi-Fi sensor, pull stock after any temperature excursion, and put responsibility in the partner agreement.
5. **Credit risk on net-15.** Card only for the first four orders. The Receivables agent chases from day 3, and the owner pauses accounts at 30 days.
6. **Plant capacity and staff.** Year 2 needs ~940 extra meals a week. The Production planner flags load at 90%. Confirm headroom before signing Elite accounts.
7. **Health claims at clinics.** No medical or GLP-1 claims on labels or in copy. The clinic's dietitian chooses the menu. J&W shares ingredient lists.
8. **Provincial licence.** The plant is Ontario-inspected, so partners must be in Ontario. Current routes already are.
9. **Growing faster than service.** Icon's 1.7 Trustpilot score shows what happens when a brand scales past its delivery capacity. Pace new accounts to route and plant capacity, and protect J&W's 4.8★.
10. **Owner-brand risk.** Partners see the brand daily. Make Lucas and the team the face of the program.
