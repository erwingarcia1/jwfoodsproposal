# CFO review of the numbers (before they go to J&W)

Reviewer: skeptical-CFO pass for Minastech · October 16, 2026 · All CAD. Every figure is an estimate.
Checked against `model-assumptions.md` (mandatory), `play-01.md` to `play-07.md`, `million.md`, `market-benchmarks.md` and `jw-audit.md`. Arithmetic was re-run line by line in a script; corrected figures are below.

## 1. Bottom line

- **The arithmetic is mostly right. The problem is the total.** As written, the seven plays add **$1.49M in year 1 and $3.67M in year 2** before overlap. That is +60% and +147% on a ~$2.5M business that has 10 to 18 people today (jw-audit.md §6). No one should present that as a plan.
- **After corrections the seven plays sum to $1.42M (year 1) and $3.58M (year 2) before overlap.** The fixes are modest one by one: churn on Goal Plans, order weeks and incrementality on Pro Partner, a smaller first holiday season and margin on the Gift Studio, and several $1M ideas pulled back inside the 0.75 to 0.85 incrementality range.
- **The year-1 plan should be five plays, not seven:** Goal Plans (1), Butcher Club (3), J&W at Work (4), Gift Studio (5) and AI Coach (6). Pro Partner (2) and Team Fuel (7) stay ready as named backups.
- **Plan total after a 28% overlap and stretch discount: about $0.80M in year 1 and $1.94M in year 2** (about $0.30M and $0.74M of gross profit). Both sit inside the believable band ($0.6 to 1.4M and $1.2 to 2.5M).
- **The $1M path still clears $1M, but with less room:** ~$1.25M a year in year 2 (was $1.29M), ~$494K gross profit (was $516K). It reaches about $1.0M annualised only at the end of 2027, and the low cases give ~$0.62M.
- **Capacity is the real constraint, and it is not costed.** By year 2 the five-play plan adds roughly 4,100 cooked items and ~300 delivery stops a week. Today J&W runs an estimated 60 to 160 delivery orders a week. A second cook and pack shift and probably a second reefer van must go into the business case.
