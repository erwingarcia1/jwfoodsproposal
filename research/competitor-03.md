Researched October 16, 2026. All figures come from the cited URL. Anything marked **ESTIMATE** is my own arithmetic or inference, with the reasoning shown.
Limit: the session's web-search budget ran out partway through, so this check did not cover social-media posts. Operating status rests on first-party pages carrying October 2026 dates, Trustpilot reviews dated October 2026, and parent-company press releases from Aug–Oct 2026.

## 1. Verdict: operating, premium, a good pick (with corrections)

**Verified operating in October 2026.**
- The **Anti-Tariff Giveaway** is live. It runs **Oct 5 to Oct 31, 2026** with a draw around Nov 5, 2026. Five winners each get an Anti-Tariff Value Box worth $279+ (https://trulocal.ca/anti-tariff-giveaway/).
- The site footer reads "© 2026". The 10-year anniversary pages are live: the "10 Years of Free Meat" offer is framed as "Start Fresh in 2026" (https://trulocal.ca/free-10yr/, https://trulocal.ca/10-year).
- Trustpilot shows reviews dated **Oct 6 and Oct 8, 2026** (https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/trulocal.ca).
- Parent EMERGE Commerce (TSXV: ECOM) published Q2 2026 results on Aug 27, 2026, a debt refinancing on Sep 23, 2026 and a debenture amendment on Oct 8, 2026 (https://www.emerge-commerce.com/). Its Jan 21, 2026 release calls truLOCAL its largest brand by revenue (https://www.emerge-commerce.com/news/News/news-details/2026/EMERGE-Provides-Corporate-Update-on-truLOCAL-as-the-Brand-Celebrates-its-10th-Anniversary).

**Premium?** Yes, at the "premium-mainstream" level: it sells named-farm, Canadian-only land proteins plus grass-fed and organic lines. It is **not** all grass-fed or organic, though. The catalog also lists AAA and AA+ graded beef and RWA (raised without antibiotics) chicken (https://trulocal.ca/products). It sells no Wagyu.

## 4. Flows worth borrowing (step by step)

**A. Subscription sign-up, 4 steps** (https://trulocal.ca/get-started/)
1. **Email + postal code** ("Find out if we serve your area"). The email is captured before the customer sees a price, which builds the abandoned-cart list. A consent line says offers will follow.
2. **Pick a box size:** Starter, Family or Mega, sized by servings and points.
3. **"Pick your Proteins":** a points-budget builder. Each product costs points, and Extra Points cover going over budget. Items can roll over to the next order.
4. **Checkout.** The free-for-life item is pre-applied when the customer arrives through a promo-coded link.

**B. Subscription self-management** (https://trulocal.ca/faq)
- My Account → Manage Subscription → skip / pause (any length) / change frequency / cancel.
- A reminder email goes out 48 h before renewal. The box locks at billing.

**C. Corporate gifting, 4 steps** (https://trulocal.ca/gifts/)
1. **Choose the boxes:** curated, value, build-your-own, or gift cards of any amount.
2. **Add a branded message.**
3. **Upload the recipient list.**
4. **truLOCAL packs and ships to each door,** from the next business day or on a scheduled date.

Supporting details:
- The quote form asks for quantity in bands: <10, 11–25, 26–50, 51–100, 100+.
- There is no minimum. "Tiered volume discounts" are mentioned, but no percentages are published.
- Contact is sales@ or a 1-800 number, with a 24-hour response promise.
- Dietary and vegetarian swaps are available on request.

**D. Converting one-time buyers.** Curated and value boxes sell with no subscription. Curated boxes carry a complimentary **$25 promo card**, which presumably works as a next-order or subscription hook (https://trulocal.ca/curated-boxes/). Value boxes show a struck-through price as an anchor.

**E. Viral contest for lead generation** (https://trulocal.ca/anti-tariff-giveaway/)
- Entry is a form plus a skill-testing question.
- Bonus entries:
  - +1 for sharing a referral link.
  - **+5 for each friend** who enters through that link.
  - +20 for a purchase.
  - +20 for a 100-word essay on local meat, which also produces user-generated content.
- The parent company owns the referral platform Viral Loops (https://www.emerge-commerce.com/).

**F. Comparison table.** Three columns: truLOCAL vs Major Grocery Stores vs Other Meat Boxes. Six rows: Canadian-sourced, fast fulfilment, free shipping, customisable, easy pause/skip/cancel, flash-frozen and vacuum-sealed. Tapping a column opens an explanation sheet (https://trulocal.ca/).

**G. Storytelling pages.** These include a 10-year timeline (first Instagram post → first box → Dragons' Den → 50K boxes → acquisition → 500K boxes) and "Meet our LOCAL partners" farm cards (https://trulocal.ca/10-year, https://trulocal.ca/).

## 6. What J&W already does better

1. **It owns an Ontario-inspected plant (since 2008)** and cuts, portions, cooks and vacuum-seals in-house (jw-facts.md). truLOCAL aggregates 100+ suppliers, and its About page names no inspection body or own plant (https://trulocal.ca/about-us/).
2. **Heritage.** The family business dates to 1973 (Advance Food Products) and J&W to 1995, versus truLOCAL's 2016 start. That supports a richer "three generations of Toronto butchers" story than a 10-year timeline.
3. **Cooked, portioned, à-la-carte Meal Butler line,** with no added salt, sugar or oil, e.g. a 4 oz cooked chicken breast for $4.20–$4.99. truLOCAL sells **no à-la-carte items** (https://trulocal.ca/faq), and its prepared range is a handful of pizzas, pierogi, pot pies, BBQ chicken and cooked chicken bites (https://trulocal.ca/products).
4. **Price per lb** (J&W catalog, jw-catalog.json):

| Item | J&W price | $/lb |
|---|---|---|
| AAA ribeye, 10 × 11–12 oz | $175 | **$23.33–25.45/lb** |
| AAA striploin case, 8 × 8–9 oz | $110 | $24.44–27.50/lb |
| Portioned chicken breast case, ~4 kg | $85 | **≈$9.64/lb** |
| Ground chicken breast | $9.99/lb | $9.99/lb |
| Wagyu/Kobe AAA ground | $14.99/lb | $14.99/lb |

   **ESTIMATE:** a J&W build of truLOCAL's Anti-Tariff box costs about **$225** at current retail. The truLOCAL box is $279 on sale and $349 regular. The J&W build:
   - 14 × 6 oz breast from the case: ≈$51
   - 7 lb ground beef at the 5 lb/$40 bulk rate: ≈$56
   - 6 lb ground chicken: ≈$60
   - 6 AAA top sirloin steaks, 7–8 oz at $10: $60

   This is **not like-for-like**: truLOCAL leans grass-fed and organic, and J&W's comparison uses AAA cuts.
5. **Premium range truLOCAL lacks:**
   - Wagyu striploin, 8–9 oz, $35
   - Canadian Prime ribeye
   - Bison and venison
   - Whole Ontario lamb ($400) and a quarter cow ($1,200)
   - Whole striploin or ribeye **cut to the customer's chosen thickness**

   truLOCAL sells fixed portions only.
6. **Shorter, controlled GTA cold chain.** Scheduled routes run from Scarborough, with free store pickup and 1–2 day express delivery in core GTA. truLOCAL relies on couriers with a 24–36 h frozen window, and thawed-box complaints recur in its Trustpilot reviews (https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/trulocal.ca).
7. **Social proof.** J&W has Google 4.8★ from 611 reviews (model-assumptions.md), versus truLOCAL's Trustpilot 3.2/5 from 68 reviews. These are different platforms, so treat the comparison as directional.
8. **Bulk and case buying** for athletes, families and food-service (55 lb chicken, 4 kg cases), plus a physical butcher counter.

**Where truLOCAL is stronger (be honest in the proposal):**
- Grass-fed and organic named-farm positioning.
- National shipping. J&W's provincially inspected meat **can only be sold within Ontario** (model-assumptions.md).
- Free shipping with no minimum. J&W has a $100 minimum plus a $20–35 route fee.
- Mature subscription and points tooling.
- A parent company with referral software (Viral Loops) and M&A appetite. EMERGE says it is hunting tuck-in competitors and corporate-gifting businesses (Jan 21, 2026 release).

## 7. Checks on the "J&W Butcher Club" play

- **Per-serving prices barely undercut truLOCAL (ESTIMATE).** The proposed Starter tier is $129 for ~30 servings = **$4.30/serving**, against truLOCAL's $4.31. Family is $229/70 = $3.27 (6% below truLOCAL's $3.49), and Freezer Fill is $299/95 = $3.15 (6% below $3.36). Add J&W's $20 route fee and J&W is at or above truLOCAL per serving at every tier: $4.97 / $3.56 / $3.36 against $4.31 / $3.49 / $3.36. Two options:
  - **Option 1:** include free route delivery for members, and size tiers at truLOCAL's serving counts (36/80/104), which puts J&W at ~$3.58/$2.86/$2.88 per serving.
  - **Option 2:** lead with **$/lb** and the AAA/Wagyu/custom-cut story instead of per-serving prices.

  Check either option against the 30% raw-box margin before publishing.
- **The "0.6% of truLOCAL's base" framing needs a caveat.** 70,100 is *cumulative customers since 2016*, not active subscribers, and the active count is not disclosed.
- **Churn.** truLOCAL's own unit economics imply ≈8 lifetime orders per customer (CLTV ~$2,000 / AOV ~$240). Model 250–400 *active* members as a steady state that needs continuous acquisition. **ESTIMATE:** at ~8 monthly orders per lifetime, about 1.5× the base must be replaced each year (12 ÷ 8), which means 375–600 new members a year to hold 250–400 active. That costs ≈$38–105K/yr at truLOCAL's CAC of $100–175, or ≈$23–72K at the $60–120 GTA CAC in model-assumptions.md.
- **The AOV benchmark supports the model.** truLOCAL's ~$240 AOV sits just above J&W's $159–229 average-box assumption, so that assumption is conservative.
- **Wild Fork timing is confirmed and stronger than stated.** **Four** Ontario stores close on or before Dec 14, 2026: Whitby, Oakville, **Ancaster** and Newmarket. Online service ran until Oct 13, 2026 (https://retail-insider.com/retail-insider/2026/10/wild-fork-exits-canada-abandoning-ambitious-ontario-expansion-plans/). Ancaster is in Hamilton, on J&W's Thursday GTA 3 route.
