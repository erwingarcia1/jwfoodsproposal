# Global best-in-class benchmarks for J&W Foods / J&W Meal Butler

Prepared for Minastech (Erwin Garcia). Research date: October 16, 2026.
Scope: operators outside the GTA whose models J&W could copy to grow 10x. The focus is cooked, portioned proteins and meals sold a la carte or in bulk to fitness customers, plus premium direct-to-consumer (DTC) meat.

**How to read this.** Every figure has a source link. A figure marked "company claim" was published by the company itself and has not been checked independently. A figure marked "estimate" is my own calculation, and the reasoning is shown. Where I could not find a number, the report says "not found."

**Research limit.** This run used up its shared web-search budget before every candidate was covered in depth. These items were not verified and are listed as not found: Omaha Steaks revenue, Huel, Thrive Market, Balance Meals, current Allen Brothers revenue, and current Crowd Cow status. A follow-up run can fill them in.

---

## 1. Executive summary: what the winners do that J&W does not

J&W already has the hard parts: an Ontario-inspected plant (since 2008), 30 years of butchery, a cooked and portioned line that has no added salt, sugar or oil, about 292 Meal Butler SKUs, and weekly GTA delivery routes. These are what Trifecta, Icon Meals and My Muscle Chef built on. The global leaders are ahead in **packaging, recurrence and distribution**, not in the food itself.

| # | Pattern among the global winners | Who proves it | J&W today |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Bulk cooked protein by the pound, plus a macro app and free coaching**, sold as the "meal prep for people who count macros" | Trifecta (>US$100M run-rate, 2021), Icon Meals ("By the Pound") | Has the products (4oz cooked chicken from $4.20), but no app, no macro panels, no coaching |
| 2 | **Subscribe & Save with skip, pause or swap**, plus a "free X for life" hook | ButcherBox ($570M+ 2025 revenue, ~450k subscribers), MegaFit (20% off), Calo (pause anytime, edit up to 48h ahead) | No subscription; customers rebuild their cart every week |
| 3 | **Gyms as a distribution and sampling channel** (gym coolers, gym retail, gym equity partners) | My Muscle Chef (grew through gym sampling to A$200M), Superfit Foods (coolers in 28 gyms), Calo and Armah Sports, Territory and Xponential, MightyMeals gym stockists | No gym program |
| 4 | **B2B and teams**: wholesale portal, pro-team partnerships, corporate smart fridges, hospitals | Icon Meals wholesale ("thousands" of partners; UFC equity), CookUnity's purchase of Fraîche (20+ NYC office fridges), Calo hospital outlets | No B2B portal, despite a family wholesale history going back to 1973 |
| 5 | **Retail and CPG adjacency** once the brand has a following | My Muscle Chef (6,000+ stockists; "retail arm is just as profitable as online"), ButcherBox in about 1,500 Target stores | Sells other brands' supplements and snacks; no J&W-branded CPG |
| 6 | **AI personalisation and AI-search content** | Calo Black (an LLM "private chef"), Territory (ML regional menus), Icon Meals' "ai-optimized" blog and AI Knowledge Base, Prep Kitchen's llms.txt | Half the catalogue has no description; no AI at all |
| 7 | **Corporate gifting and premium boxes** sold at set price tiers | Omaha Steaks (5,000+ business clients since 2020), Snake River Farms (gift bundles grouped by price; quarter beef at $1,399) | Has gift boxes ($89.99 to $199) and a quarter cow ($1,200), but they are hard to find and there is no corporate program |

**Timely local opening.** JBS-owned **Wild Fork is leaving Canada.** Its Ontario online service ended October 13, 2026, and its stores in **Whitby, Oakville, Ancaster and Newmarket** close by December 14, 2026 ([Retail Insider](https://retail-insider.com/retail-insider/2026/10/wild-fork-exits-canada-abandoning-ambitious-ontario-expansion-plans/)). Whitby, Oakville and Newmarket are all on J&W's existing delivery routes (GTA 1, GTA 3 and GTA 4). Wild Fork's premium frozen-meat customers in those towns are looking for a new supplier right now.

## 2. Ranked benchmarks (best model for J&W to copy first)

### 1. Trifecta Nutrition (Sacramento, USA): the closest model to Meal Butler
- **What it is:** Organic, macro-balanced prepared meals, plus an **a la carte "Meal Prep" line of bulk cooked proteins, carbs and vegetables**, a macro-tracking app and nutrition coaching.
- **Scale:** US$20M Series B in August 2021, led by Spring Lake Equity Partners. The company was **already profitable** at an **annual run-rate above US$100M**, with average revenue growth over 145% a year for the previous four years ([Private Equity Wire](https://www.privateequitywire.co.uk/trifecta-raises-usd20m-series-b-round-led-spring-lake-equity-partners/)). Total funding is about US$23.9M ([Dealroom via search](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/trifectanutrition)). It has 4.6 stars from 1.5k+ Google reviews ([trifectanutrition.com](https://www.trifectanutrition.com/)). Co-branded meals with CrossFit were announced in 2022 (search summary of company coverage).
- **Pricing (a la carte bulk, current shop):** Grilled and sous-vide chicken breast 16oz (4 servings) **US$14.99**. Grilled salmon 16oz $23.99. Grass-fed bison burger 16oz $26.99. Grass-fed sirloin about 4oz $23.99 (as listed). White rice and sweet potato mash 16oz $9.99. Broccoli 16oz $10.99. Green beans 16oz $11.99. **Order minimum $99** ([Trifecta shop](https://shop.trifectanutrition.com/shop/category/meal-prep/)). Prepared meals run about **$13.49 to $16.99 per meal** ([MealFan](https://mealfan.com/how-much-does-trifecta-cost/)), with about $9.99 shipping per box (review sites).
- **Subscription mechanics:** Change or skip deliveries on the web or in the app, with changes due by the Friday before delivery ([Healthline](https://healthline.com/nutrition/trifecta-meals)). Pause for up to 4 weeks ([Active.com](https://www.active.com/nutrition/articles/trifecta-review)).
- **App:** The free "Trifecta" food-logging and macro-tracking app has premium at **$9.99 a month or $49.99 a year** and a 14-day trial ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/trifecta/id492100601)). The app itself is a lead-generation and retention engine.
- **Why it is premium:** Organic, grass-fed and wild-caught sourcing, athlete endorsements, and macro precision.
- **Comparison with J&W:** J&W's cooked chicken is 4oz from C$4.20, which is about **C$16.80/lb** (estimate: $4.20 × 4). That is roughly on par with Trifecta's US$14.99/lb once you convert currency. J&W's no-salt, no-sugar, no-oil claim is actually stricter than Trifecta's.
- **Best idea for J&W:** Repackage Meal Butler as **"Macro Packs"**: 1lb (4×4oz) bulk packs of protein, carbs and vegetables with printed macros, a $99 minimum, and a free macro-calculator and tracker (web app first). Then offer a free 15-minute coaching call with every first order.

### 2. Icon Meals (Dallas, USA): bulk "By the Pound", plus wholesale to gyms and teams
- **What it is:** Prepared meals, custom-built meals, **bulk cooked proteins by the pound**, protein snacks (popcorn, protein butters, coffee) and seasonings ([iconmeals.com](https://iconmeals.com/)).
- **Scale:** Revenue was **over US$14M in 2023**, with 75 employees ([Garage Gym Reviews](https://www.garagegymreviews.com/icon-meals-review)). The company claims **over 12 million meals sold** ([iconmeals.com](https://iconmeals.com/)). CB Insights lists US$7.09M raised ([CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/icon-meals)). In 2021 **UFC became its Official Meal Delivery Partner and bought an equity stake**, citing 56% annualised growth since 2014 ([UFC.com](https://www.ufc.com/news/ufc-announces-major-partnership-icon-meals)).
- **Pricing:** About **$6.00 to $12.50 per meal**. Custom meals start at $6. The 12-meal plan is about $12 per meal and the 24-meal plan about $11. There is an 8-meal minimum, and military and first responders get 15% off ([Garage Gym Reviews](https://www.garagegymreviews.com/icon-meals-review)). Per-lb bulk prices: not found.
- **B2B arm:** The wholesale page targets **stores, gyms, weight-loss clinics, training centres, offices and employee-benefit programs**. Orders run from "a few dozen meals" up to thousands a week. It lists partners including UFC, the US Olympic Team, the Dallas Cowboys and USA Triathlon. There is a separate wholesale login portal, and the company says it handles meat processing in-house in a USDA facility ([Icon wholesale](https://iconmeals.com/pages/wholesale)). Icon also pitches office fridges stocked with its meals as a **replacement for vending machines** ([Icon blog](https://iconmeals.com/blogs/ai-optimized/corporate-wellness-vending-machines-vs-icon-meals)).
- **AI usage:** Icon runs a whole blog section at `/blogs/ai-optimized/`. Its posts are question-titled, answer first, use headings and tables, and close on the brand, which is a clear generative-engine-optimisation (GEO) strategy for AI search. Examples include "Bulk meat by the pound: is it cheaper?" and GLP-1 meal-prep guides ([Icon blog](https://iconmeals.com/blogs/ai-optimized/bulk-meat-by-the-pound-is-it-cheaper)). The footer also links an "AI Knowledge Base."
- **Best idea for J&W:** Open a **J&W Wholesale and Teams portal** (gyms, trainers, physio and weight-loss clinics, OHL and university teams) with a low minimum, such as 8 meals or 1 case. Run it alongside an **AI-optimised content hub** so ChatGPT and Google AI answers recommend "J&W Meal Butler" for "meal prep Toronto" and "cooked chicken by the pound Toronto."

### 3. My Muscle Chef (Sydney, Australia): gym sampling to A$200M, then retail
- **Scale:** First-year revenue was **A$827k**. Revenue was A$26M in 2018 and **A$200M in 2022**. The company has 500+ staff and makes 2.2M+ meals, snacks and drinks a month ([C&I / c-store.com.au, Dec 2023](https://www.c-store.com.au/?p=62079)). It opened a **A$55M, 10,000 sqm facility** with weekly capacity of 1.2M meals ([realcommercial.com.au](https://www.realcommercial.com.au/news/from-humble-home-kitchen-to-55m-factory-the-rapid-rise-of-my-muscle-chef)). It is backed by Quadrant Private Equity ([Just Food](https://www.just-food.com/news/australias-my-muscle-chef-gets-private-equity-investment)).
- **How it grew:** Through **word of mouth and gym sampling**. It started paid online ads only in its fourth year. It pioneered vacuum-sealed ready meals in Australia ([Food & Drink Business](https://www.foodanddrinkbusiness.com.au/news/muscling-in-on-meals)).
- **Retail and wholesale:** It has **6,000+ retail stockists** (Woolworths, Coles, fuel stations). Protein cookies (2021) opened up convenience retail. The CEO says "our retail arm is just as profitable as online" ([c-store.com.au](https://www.c-store.com.au/?p=62079)).
- **Best idea for J&W:** Run a **gym sampling and ambassador program**: free sampling days at 20 GTA gyms a month and trainer codes. Then add a **J&W-branded high-protein CPG line** (jerky, protein egg bites, bone broth) for gym fridges and independent grocers.

### 4. Prep Kitchen (Kent, UK): bootstrapped high-protein meal prep with simple plans
- **Scale:** Ranked **#27 on the 2024 Sunday Times 100** with sales of **£13.9M and 138.7% growth**. It also appeared on the 2025 list ([search summary of the Sunday Times listing and Prep Kitchen's announcement](https://prepkitchen.co.uk/blog/news/prep-kitchen-achieves-a-place-in-the-2024-the-sunday-times-100-list)). FEBE reports **£22.8M sales in 2024** ([FEBE G100](https://www.febe.com/2025_g100/prep-kitchen/)). The company claims £40M+ revenue (company claim, conflicts with the figures above). It has 20,000+ active weekly subscribers and makes nearly 100,000 meals a week from a 50,000 sq ft Kent site (company and press claims, via search). The founders started with £10k in 2015, repaid it within a year, and the business has been **cash-flow funded** since ([search summary](https://www.thebusinessdesk.com/northwest/?p=2101539)).
- **Pricing:** **£6.95 per meal**, cancel anytime, free delivery ([Prep Kitchen homepage](https://prepkitchen.co.uk/homepage-comp)). By plan: Fat Loss £6.25 balanced / £7 low-carb, and Muscle Gain £7.50 / £8.25 ([CoachWeb](https://coachweb.com/health/nutrition/prep-kitchen-review-tasty-balanced-meals-for-active-people)). Meals carry up to 60g protein ([Prep Kitchen menu](https://prepkitchen.co.uk/menu)).
- **AI usage:** It publishes an `llms.txt` file to guide AI crawlers ([prepkitchen.co.uk/llms.txt](https://prepkitchen.co.uk/llms.txt)).
- **Best idea for J&W:** Cut the choice down to **3 goal-based plans** (Fat Loss, Muscle Gain, Maintenance), each with a set weekly price and regular and large portions. The deep 9-category mega-menu then becomes the place for add-ons. Bootstrapped growth to about £14 to 23M shows this works without venture capital.

### 5. Calo (Riyadh, GCC and UK): AI-personalised meal subscriptions
- **Scale:** **280K customers in the GCC and 18M meals delivered** (company claim, [calo.app](https://calo.app/en-sa)). It had nine-figure (US$) annualised revenue, 10M+ meals in 2024 and 50%+ year-on-year growth in H1 2025. It raised a **US$64M Series B** (a $25M round in December 2024 plus a $39M extension in July 2025). It has 10+ physical sites, **including hospital-based outlets**, and is planning a Saudi IPO ([Wamda](https://www.wamda.com/2025/07/calo-raises-39-milliion-series-b-extension-boost-global-growth)). In 2025 it **acquired the UK brands Fresh Fitness Food and Detox Kitchen** ([CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/fresh-fitness-food/financials)).
- **Pricing:** From **SAR 31 per meal and SAR 23 per breakfast**. Delivery is 5, 6 or 7 days a week, in morning or evening slots ([calo.app](https://calo.app/en-sa)).
- **Subscription mechanics:** No contracts. Pause anytime by picking dates in the app. Skip, swap, remove ingredients or edit meals up to 48 hours before delivery. Plans are High Protein, Balanced, Vegetarian, Low Carb and **Custom Macros**, where you enter your own targets ([calo.app](https://calo.app/en-sa); [Calo help](https://help.calo.app/en/articles/4704801-how-to-pause-the-subscription-plan)).
- **AI usage:** It is piloting **"Calo Black", an AI private chef that uses LLMs to learn preferences through conversation and generate daily menus**, and is building AI into customer service and operations ([Wamda](https://www.wamda.com/2025/07/calo-raises-39-milliion-series-b-extension-boost-global-growth)).
- **B2B and gyms:** A strategic partnership with premium gym operator **Armah Sports** covers co-located retail outlets, cross-promotion and distribution through gym networks. Armah's founder joined Calo's board ([Wamda](https://www.wamda.com/2025/07/calo-raises-39-milliion-series-b-extension-boost-global-growth)).
- **Best idea for J&W:** Build a **"Meal Butler AI": a chat concierge** that asks for goal, weight, training and dislikes, then builds a weekly cart from the 292 cooked SKUs to hit the customer's macros. Pair it with a **Custom Macros** plan.

### 6. ButcherBox (Boston, USA): the subscription-meat benchmark
- **Scale:** **2025 revenue above US$570M, up more than 10%**, with about **450,000 US subscribers**. It launched in about **1,500 Target stores** in January 2026 with five fresh grass-fed beef items at $8 to $14/lb ([Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/01/15/business/butcherbox-target-stores-meat-retail); [Nasdaq PR](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/butcherbox-launches-target-2026-01-15)). It is **bootstrapped**: a ~$210k Kickstarter, and it has **never raised outside capital** ([Built In](https://www.builtinboston.com/articles/story-behind-butcherbox); [BU](https://www.bu.edu/articles/2023/my-big-idea-mike-salguero-butcherbox/)). It also sells through BJ's, Instacart, DoorDash DashMarket and Target Plus ([Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/operations/butcherbox-launches-on-targets-marketplace-in-subscription-free-acquisition-play/)).
- **Pricing:** **US$8.52 to $15.23/lb** (Signature Medium, effective January 8, 2026) ([butcherbox.com](https://www.butcherbox.com/)). Curated boxes run about $99 to $189 ([Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/operations/butcherbox-launches-on-targets-marketplace-in-subscription-free-acquisition-play/)).
- **Subscription mechanics:** Choose Essentials (6 items) or Signature (6, 9 or 12 items). Swap among 100+ cuts until the night before billing. Skip or cancel anytime, with free shipping. **Sign-up hook: "FREE chicken breasts for life", or free NY strips or filets for a year**, worth up to US$422. Weekly member deals and **2% back in rewards points**. It shows **70k+ five-star reviews** and is B Corp certified ([butcherbox.com](https://www.butcherbox.com/)).
- **Lesson:** People who left often did so because "people didn't want a subscription." That is why ButcherBox added one-off retail and marketplace sales ([Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/operations/butcherbox-launches-on-targets-marketplace-in-subscription-free-acquisition-play/)).
- **Best idea for J&W:** Launch the **"J&W Butcher's Box"**, a monthly subscription for the raw side (AAA, Ontario air-chilled chicken, Ontario lamb) with a **"free 4oz cooked chicken pack in every box for life"** hook. Keep one-off ordering for customers who will not subscribe.

### 7. CookUnity (New York, USA, and Toronto): the chef marketplace already in J&W's market
- **Scale:** **ARR above US$750M** in 2025, **43M+ meals** (up 80%+), 200k+ eaters, average chef earning about US$850k a year ([FSR Magazine](https://www.fsrmagazine.com/industry-news/cookunity-closes-strongest-year-yet/)). It has **up to US$250M in non-dilutive financing** from General Catalyst and runs 8 kitchens ([Food On Demand](https://foodondemand.com/12032025/cookunity-scales-up-with-250m-in-funding-connecting-chefs-to-home-diners/)).
- **Canada:** It bought Toronto's Cookin and **launched in Ontario/Toronto in June 2025** with chefs including Patrick Kriss (Alo). Meals are fresh, never frozen, and delivered within 24 hours ([Retail Insider](https://retail-insider.com/retail-insider/2025/06/cookunity-launches-in-canada-with-top-chefs-to-redefine-meal-delivery/); [BetaKit](https://betakit.com/cookin-acquired-by-us-based-fellow-chef-made-meal-delivery-startup-cookunity/)).
- **Pricing (US):** 4 meals at $13.59 per serving, 6 at $12.29, 8 at $11.69, plus a $9.99 delivery fee ([Healthline](https://www.healthline.com/nutrition/cookunity-reviews)). Plans run 4 to 16 meals a week.
- **B2B:** It **acquired Fraîche** (May 2025), a smart-fridge platform with 20+ NYC office locations, 10k+ users and 500k+ meals served. Clients include PayPal and BCG, and the Empire State Building fridge sells 200+ meals a day. Users unlock the fridge by scanning in the app ([search summary of Business Wire release](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250514671018/en/CookUnity-Expands-Its-Chef-Led-Offering-Into-the-Workplace-with-Acquisition-of-Fra%C3%AEche)). It also has an Airbnb partnership, with Canada expansion planned for 2026 ([FSR](https://www.fsrmagazine.com/industry-news/cookunity-closes-strongest-year-yet/)).
- **Best idea for J&W:** Put **J&W smart fridges (unlocked by app or tap-to-pay) in GTA gyms, offices and condo buildings**, stocked with Meal Butler pre-set meals at $8.99 to $14.99. This is the Fraîche model, and CookUnity does not yet run it in Toronto.

### 8. Clean Eatz (Wilmington NC, USA): franchised pickup meal plans with 5 revenue streams
- **Scale:** **116 locations** in 20+ states with about 65 in development. **AUV slightly above US$1M.** The Clean Eatz Kitchen DTC arm brings in close to **US$28M**, and it has a **US Marine Corps contract** ([Food On Demand](https://foodondemand.com/03182026/outstanding-operator-clean-eatz/)). Meal plans plus grab-and-go make up about **75% of system sales**. The 5 revenue streams are meal plans, grab-and-go, cafe dining, catering and retail ([Franchising.com](https://www.franchising.com/news/20260709_clean_eatz_reports_strong_q1_as_fiverevenuestream_model_fuels_franchise_gro.html)). Average franchise unit revenue is about $1.087M with a 6% royalty ([Franzy](https://franzy.com/franchises/clean-eatz)).
- **Pricing:** **Tiered per-meal pricing**: about $8.79 to $8.99 up to 9 meals, $7.89 to $8.29 for 10 to 14, $7.59 to $7.99 for 15 to 20, and $6.99 to $7.39 for 21+. Extra protein is +$1.75. Order online by Sunday night and pick up at a local cafe ([Clean Eatz guide](https://cleaneatz.com/blog/clean-eatz-meal-plan-guide-20260614)).
- **Tech:** Punchh loyalty integrated with Brink POS. About 80% of franchise sales are dine-in or pickup ([Food On Demand](https://foodondemand.com/03182026/outstanding-operator-clean-eatz/)).
- **Best idea for J&W:** Use **volume-tier pricing**, where the per-meal price drops at 10, 15 and 21 meals, and turn the Brimley store into a **Sunday-cutoff pickup hub** with grab-and-go fridges. Later, license "Meal Butler pickup points" to gyms.

### 9. Superfit Foods, owned by Muscle Maker Inc (Florida, USA): gym-cooler pickup network
- **Model:** Subscribers **picked up meals and juices from coolers in 28 partner gyms and wellness centres**, restocked twice a week. Home delivery and national shipping were also available. It made **220,000+ meals in 2020** and had a 150+ meal menu ([QSR / NRN coverage via search](https://www.nrn.com/fast-casual/muscle-maker-grill-buys-florida-meal-prep-service-superfit-foods-for-525-000-as-part-of-expansion)). Muscle Maker bought it in 2021, reportedly for about US$525k (one source).
- **Status:** As of October 2026, superfitfoods.com redirects to a GoDaddy "domain for sale" page. **The business appears to be inactive** (observed directly).
- **Best idea for J&W:** Copy the **gym-cooler pickup model**, which cuts last-mile cost because one drop serves many customers, but run it under J&W's own brand with its own route density. Superfit was a small acquired brand; J&W already has the plant and the routes.

### 10. Factor, owned by HelloFresh (USA and Ontario): mass-market ready-to-eat
- **Scale:** HelloFresh bought Factor in 2020 for up to US$277M. Management said Factor grew from about a €100M to a **€1B+ revenue run-rate** (2023 CEO remark, via [Think Insights](https://thinkinsights.net/strategy/hellofresh-chooses-profit-over-growth)). HelloFresh group revenue was €6.76B in 2025, down 11.8% ([HelloFresh Annual Report 2025](https://www.lobbyregister.bundestag.de/media/ff/cf/781215/Annual-Report-2025.pdf)). Factor's own revenue was not found.
- **Product:** In September 2025 it expanded to **100+ weekly options**, doubled its high-protein options, extended the **GLP-1 Support line** and offers 65+ add-ons a week ([Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250908230666/en)). It launched a "Factor Form" protein meal shake in June 2026 ([BoxRox](https://www.boxrox.com/factor-form-debuts-protein-packed-meal-shake-designed-to-support-weight-loss-and-muscle-retention/)). Its own marketing says subscriptions include **two free registered-dietitian sessions** ([Factor](https://www.factor75.com/stories/research/best-glp1-meal-delivery-reviews)).
- **Canada:** It ships in Ontario. Meals cost from about C$15 each (6 meals) to about C$12 (18 meals), plus about C$10 shipping ([Flavour Network](https://flavournetwork.ca/article/factor-meals-review-canada)).
- **Best idea for J&W:** Create a **"GLP-1 Companion" menu**: small, protein-dense 3 to 4oz portions with fibre-rich sides and no added sugar. This fits J&W's existing no-salt, no-sugar, no-oil line, and Ozempic and Mounjaro users need high protein in small volumes.

### 11. Territory Foods (Washington DC, USA): local-chef network plus a fitness-franchisor partnership
- **Scale:** **US$44M raised** in total, including a US$22M round in 2021 led by US Venture Partners ([Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20210413005160/en/4954437/Territory-Foods-Raises-22M-in-Funding-Led-by-U.S.-Venture-Partners)). Owler estimates revenue at US$25 to 100M (estimate, [Owler](https://www.owler.com/company/territoryfoods)). It offers 35+ weekly options, still live in 2026 ([territoryfoods.com](https://www.territoryfoods.com/)).
- **B2B:** It became **Xponential Fitness's first official prepared-meals partner**, with separate menus for Pure Barre, Row House, Rumble and StretchLab, accessed through a tile in each brand's member app, so the studios need "no retail footprint" ([Franchising.com](https://www.franchising.com/news/20230120_xponential_fitness_partners_with_territory_foods_to_optimize_health_results.html)).
- **AI usage:** It uses "proprietary data science and machine learning" to choose regional menus by taste, lifestyle and protein trends (same source).

### 12. Fresh Fitness Food (London, UK): bespoke macros, now owned by Calo
- **Model:** It calculates each client's macros from height, weight and weekly training. For example, Fat Loss uses a 20% calorie deficit with 40% protein, 20% carbs and 40% fat. It delivers daily meal bags in London, about 1,000 bags a day (company claim) ([City Matters](https://citymatters.london/counting-macros-fresh-fitness-food-math)).
- **Pricing:** From about £23 a day for 3 meals ([Women's Fitness](https://womensfitness.co.uk/?p=7585)). Another listing gives £140 to £215 for 5 days ([sp-edge](https://sp-edge.com/companies/350092)).
- **Exit:** Acquired by Calo in April 2025 ([CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/fresh-fitness-food/financials)). It also sells through employee-benefit platforms ([Thanks Ben](https://www.thanksben.com/benefits-providers/fresh-fitness-food)). Revenue was not found.
- **Best idea for J&W:** Offer a **premium "Personal Macro Plan" tier** at a set daily price, with portions weighed to the customer's own macros. J&W already weighs every portion, so this mostly means printing a personal label.

### 13. Wild Fork Foods, owned by JBS (USA): blast-frozen premium meat stores, now leaving Ontario
- **Scale:** About **60 to 64 US stores**: 64 per [ExchangeRight](https://www.exchangeright.com/tenant-profiles/wild-fork-foods/) and 60 per [Sentient Media, May 2026](https://sentientmedia.org/when-your-local-meat-market-is-owned-by-jbs/). Products are vacuum-sealed and frozen at -40 ([Tampa Bay Beacons](https://www.tampabaybeacons.com/2026/08/25/wild-fork-foods-to-replace-seminole-boston-market/)).
- **Canada exit:** It entered in 2023 with plans for about 20 GTA sites and later aimed for about 30 Ontario stores. It is now closing **Whitby, Oakville, Ancaster and Newmarket**: online service ended October 13 and stores close by December 14, 2026. The Canadian range was 600+ products, and a Sobeys store-in-store had opened in Brantford in 2026 ([Retail Insider](https://retail-insider.com/retail-insider/2026/10/wild-fork-exits-canada-abandoning-ambitious-ontario-expansion-plans/)).
- **Best idea for J&W:** Run a **"Welcome, Wild Fork shoppers" campaign now**: geo-targeted ads in Whitby, Oakville and Newmarket, a first-box discount, and an emphasis on J&W's existing Tuesday, Thursday and Friday routes to those towns. J&W's flash-frozen, vacuum-sealed format is the same thing those customers are used to.

Tiered promotions (spend $250 save $50, up to $400 save $120). Subscribe & Save "Build Your Box". **Gift bundles grouped by price** (<$200, $200 to $300, >$300). Group and corporate gifting ([snakeriverfarms.com](https://www.snakeriverfarms.com/)). Revenue was not found.

### 19. Cautionary tales: what not to copy
- **Porter Road (Nashville):** A whole-animal butcher that moved online. It raised a US$3.7M seed and a US$10M Series A to build a larger USDA plant in Kentucky ([TechCrunch via search](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2122352)). It **filed Chapter 11 on March 17, 2025** (case 3:25-bk-01134; liabilities of $1 to 10M) and closed its flagship shop on June 14, 2025. It continues online and through grocers ([Inforuptcy](https://www.inforuptcy.com/browse-filings/tennessee-middle-bankruptcy-court/3:25-bk-01134/bankruptcy-case-porter-road-butcher-meat-co-llc); [Nashville Post](https://www.nashvillepost.com/business/legal/porter-road-owners-declare-bankruptcy/article_bc085704-cc11-4762-a737-5015d2676cd1.html)). Lesson: do not over-build capacity ahead of demand.
- **Farmison & Co (UK):** A premium online butcher with FY21 sales of £12M (company claim). It went into insolvency proceedings in 2023, and in 2025 the business was split up: the plant went to GPS Food Group and the brand and website to Donald Russell ([search summary of The Grocer / Food Manufacture](https://thestrayferret.co.uk/ripon-online-butcher-acquired)). Lesson: a premium DTC brand needs recurring revenue and B2B ballast.
- **Snap Kitchen (Texas):** Closed **more than half its stores (20+)** in November 2020 and shifted to subscription delivery and Whole Foods counters ([Restaurant Business](https://restaurantbusinessonline.com/operations/snap-kitchen-close-majority-its-stores)). Lesson: grow retail through partners' shelves and fridges, not company-owned leases.
- **Crowd Cow (Seattle):** A "crowdfund a cow" marketplace. It raised about US$25M in total and claimed more than US$1M a month in revenue early on ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=1645280); [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2019/seattle-startup-crowd-cow-raising-cash-beef-meat-marketplace/)). Current status was not verified. Idea worth borrowing: **"cowpooling"**, where neighbours split a quarter cow, which suits J&W's $1,200 quarter cow.

## 3. Cross-cutting playbook for J&W

### Subscription mechanics (copy all of these)
- **Choice of rhythm:** weekly, bi-weekly or monthly. ButcherBox offers 2, 4, 6 or 8 weeks ([Hip2Keto](https://hip2keto.com/butcher-box-price/)).
- **Self-serve skip, pause and swap** until a cutoff: 48h at Calo, the night before billing at ButcherBox, the Friday before at Trifecta.
- **Default discount of 5 to 20%** for subscribing (MegaFit 20%), **volume tiers** (Clean Eatz), and a **"free X for life" hook** (ButcherBox).
- **A one-off option alongside subscriptions:** ButcherBox's exit research found that many cancellations came from people who did not want a subscription.
- **Points and referrals:** 2% back (ButcherBox); referral credit for both sides, $30 each at ButcherBox according to a coupon tracker ([GrabOn](https://grabon.com/butcher-box-coupons)).

### B2B arms that work
| Channel | Proof | J&W fit |
|---|---|---|
| Gym coolers and fridges | Superfit (28 gyms), Calo and Armah, MightyMeals, My Muscle Chef sampling | Very high: J&W's buyers are already gym-goers ("Eat clean, train mean") |
| Teams and athletes | Icon (UFC equity, Dallas Cowboys, US Olympic Team) | High: OHL, university and college varsity teams, and combat gyms in the GTA |
| Corporate fridges | CookUnity and Fraîche (20+ NYC offices) | Medium-high: return-to-office in downtown Toronto |
| Clinics and hospitals | Icon (weight-loss clinics), Calo (hospital outlets) | High: GLP-1 clinics, bariatric programs |
| Corporate gifting | Omaha Steaks (5,000+ business clients) | High, and seasonal (Q4) |
| Grocery and retail | My Muscle Chef (6,000+ stockists), ButcherBox and Target | Later stage, once there is J&W-branded CPG |

### AI used by the leaders
- **Conversational planning:** Calo Black, an LLM private chef that builds daily menus from conversation.
- **ML menu selection by region:** Territory Foods.
- **AI-search content:** Icon Meals' "ai-optimized" blog and AI Knowledge Base, and Prep Kitchen's llms.txt.
- **Self-serve plan management in the app:** Calo, Trifecta.
- I found no public evidence of AI features at ButcherBox, CookUnity or Factor. That leaves room for a smaller operator to stand out.
