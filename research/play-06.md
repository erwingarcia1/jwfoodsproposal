## 1. What Calo does, and what we borrow

Calo sells subscription meal plans in Saudi Arabia, the Gulf and, since 2025, the UK (it bought Fresh Fitness Food in April 2025 and Detox Kitchen now trades as Calo). Its sharpest idea is **Custom Macros**: a short stepper (birthday, sex, height, weight, activity, goal) calculates calories, protein, carbs and fat, the customer can edit them, and the plan is cooked to those numbers. Custom Macros meals cost about 32% more than its Balanced meals (SAR 47.50 vs SAR 36 for lunch or dinner, incl. VAT, per its plans-page config). Customers can skip, pause, swap or edit meals up to 48 hours ahead in the GCC (one business day in the UK). It offers a 15–20 minute nutritionist call, and in July 2025 it announced a pilot of "Calo Black", an LLM private chef that learns preferences through conversation (no public product found in Oct 2026).

**Proof it works (company claims and press):** 280K GCC customers and 18M meals (KSA page; the global page now says 290K and 19M), 10M+ meals in 2024, 50%+ year-on-year growth in H1 2025, a US$64M Series B, and 12 café locations including one inside a hospital ([Wamda](https://www.wamda.com/2025/07/calo-raises-39-milliion-series-b-extension-boost-global-growth); [calo.app](https://calo.app/en-sa); competitor-06.md). Calo has no Canadian presence.

**Ideas J&W borrows (rebuilt in J&W's voice):**
1. Goal intake that ends in editable daily macros, with the maths shown.
2. A plan priced above the standard menu because it is cooked to your numbers.
3. Self-serve skip, pause and swap before a cut-off (J&W's is the Sunday order deadline).
4. A conversational AI that builds the week, with a free human call behind it.
5. Macro dots on every dish card and a sticky live summary while you build.

**Ideas that are J&W's own (not verified at Calo):** your name, the day and the macros on every bag (the Fresh Fitness Food label idea could not be verified, since freshfitnessfood.com no longer resolves); an InBody scan at the Brimley Rd store every 4 weeks (the One Life Meals idea, $25 a scan, [onelifemeals.com](https://www.onelifemeals.com/)); Instagram DMs as the front door (30K followers).

**Where J&W wins:** its own Ontario-inspected plant since 2008 and a family butcher line back to the 1960s; portions sold by the ounce with no plan minimum; no added salt, sugar or oil; flash-frozen, so one weekly route covers much of Ontario; a store customers can walk into for a scan; 4.8★ from 611 Google reviews; and a built day at roughly $29–$33 against about $42 (Balanced) to $57 (Custom Macros) at Calo (competitor-06.md §6, estimate).

## 2. The mechanism (what J&W changes)

1. **A free coach.** "Ask the Butler" on jwfoods.com and in Instagram DMs (DM the word PLAN to @jwfoods) asks seven questions: goal, age/sex/height/weight, training days, which meals, foods you skip, weekly budget and city. It calculates daily targets with dietitian-approved rules and builds Monday to Friday from real Meal Butler SKUs. Example for a 38-year-old woman, 165 cm, 78 kg, training 3 days, losing fat: **1,610 kcal · 140 g protein · 160 g carbs · 45 g fat (est.)**. Monday lunch is Chicken Breast Souvlaki 175 g + White Basmati Rice 200 g + Broccoli 110 g; breakfast is Egg White Bites, Spinach & Feta ($5.25) + Steel Cut Oats ($4.40).
2. **Buy it two ways.** À la carte in J&W's standard 4oz and 6oz sizes (that whole default week is **$159.12** at today's prices), or as a **Personal Macro Plan** where each portion is weighed to your grams and packed as a meal with a personal label.
3. **The Personal Macro Plan (Concept).** Lunch & Dinner (10 meals) $119 / $139 / $159 or Full Day (15 meals) $169 / $189 / $219 a week, by size (Regular up to 120 g protein a day, Plus 121–160 g, Performance over 160 g). Included: gram-weighed meals, your name and macros on every bag, a weekly check-in the AI drafts and a person at J&W approves, an InBody scan at 2201 Brimley Rd every 4 weeks, and a free 15-minute call with a partner registered dietitian to start. Skip or pause before the Sunday cut-off; no contract. Delivery is J&W's normal route fee, or free pickup.
4. **Re-plan loop.** Every 4 weeks the scan result and the client's feedback go to the check-in agent, which drafts new grams and swaps within the dietitian's limits; Lucas (or the coach desk) approves before anything changes.
5. **Safety first.** Any mention of diabetes, kidney disease, pregnancy, allergies, eating-disorder history, medications or an age under 18 stops the automatic plan and routes the person to a human or the partner dietitian.
6. **Production.** At the Sunday cut-off the production agent turns confirmed plans into a Saturday weigh-and-pack sheet and a label print run. Personal meals ride on the existing route days.

**Standing orders created from the coach (Subscribe & Save) are counted in Play 1 (Goal Plan Builder), not here.** This play counts only the Personal Macro Plan, the extra value of coach-built one-time carts, and walk-in scans.

## 4. The math

### Shared assumptions (model-assumptions.md)
- Gross margin 45% (cooked and portioned). Incrementality 0.75–0.85. CAC $60–120. Churn 6–8% (standing order, best) to 8–15% (meal kit).
- 4.33 weeks a month. Clients are active 40 of 52 weeks (0.77) because of skips, pauses and holidays.
- **Net factor 0.70 on Personal Macro Plan revenue:** about 40% of clients will be existing customers who already spend roughly $90 a week (so only ~49% of their plan is new), and about 15% of new clients would otherwise have joined Play 1. 0.6 × 0.85 + 0.4 × 0.49 ≈ 0.70.
- **$33 incremental per coach-built one-time cart:** 30% of these carts are new customers ($130 AOV × 0.6 incrementality = $23.40) and 70% are existing customers who add about $18 of breakfasts and sides (× 0.8 = $10.08).
- PMP monthly churn 9% (between standing-order and meal-kit benchmarks).

New PMP clients needed: about 9–10 a month (roughly 1% of coach conversations) to grow to ~60 enrolled while 9% churn a month.

**Year 1 (mid):**
- Personal Macro Plan: 380 client-months × 4.33 × 0.77 = 1,267 paid weeks × $170 = $215,383 gross × 0.70 = **$150,768**
- Coach-built carts: 1,045 × $33 = **$34,485**
- Walk-in scans: 230 × $25 = **$5,750**
- **Total ≈ $191,000**

**Low:** 60% of the clients at $160 a week, 70% of carts and scans = 760 paid weeks × $160 × 0.70 = $85,140 + $24,140 + $4,025 = **$113,300**.
**High:** 140% of the clients at $180, 130% of carts and scans = 1,774 × $180 × 0.70 = $223,491 + $44,830 + $7,475 = **$275,800**.

### Run-rate (year 2)
- PMP: 70 enrolled on average (60 → 80) × 52 × 0.77 × $170 = $476,476 gross × 0.70 = **$333,533**
- Coach-built carts: 180 a month × 12 × $33 = **$71,280**
- Walk-in scans: 40 a month × 12 × $25 = **$12,000**
- **Total ≈ $417,000** (low case at 50 clients ≈ $299K; high case at 90 clients ≈ $535K)

### Profit after JW's own costs
- Year 1: 45% × $191K = $86K gross profit − $36K play costs (setup + monthly) ≈ **$50K contribution** before Minastech's fee.
- Year 2: 45% × $417K = $188K − ~$42K ≈ **$146K contribution**.
- Upside not counted: repeat orders from coach-acquired new customers, store purchases on scan visits, raw-meat add-ons to plan deliveries.

### Sanity check
J&W mid-case revenue is ~$2.5M (range $1.2–4.0M). $191K is about 7.6% of that in year 1, inside the usual $60–450K for one play. 60 enrolled clients is 0.2% of the 30K Instagram audience.

## 10. Risks and how we handle them

- **Health advice.** The coach gives general nutrition guidance only. A registered dietitian signs off the rules; conditions, medications, pregnancy, eating-disorder signals and under-18s go to a person. Staff are "coaches"; only the partner uses "registered dietitian" (a protected title in Ontario).
- **Macro accuracy and label claims.** J&W publishes no nutrition panels today. Keep "estimate" wording, lab-test top sellers, and confirm personal-label content and "salt and sugar free" claims with a labelling consultant (CFIA / Health Canada).
- **Kitchen complexity.** Gram-weighed meals add pack time and room for error. Cap the plan at ~100 clients until the weigh-and-pack workflow is proven; one fixed Saturday run.
- **Overlap.** Some plan clients are existing buyers or would have joined Play 1. Handled with the 0.70 net factor and by counting coach standing orders in Play 1.
- **Privacy.** Weight, body composition and GLP-1 use are sensitive personal information under PIPEDA. Get clear consent, keep only what the plan needs, never use health details for ad targeting, and let people delete their data.
- **AI mistakes.** The coach reads only approved data; prices come from Shopify; every plan and change is approved by a person; transcripts are sampled weekly.
- **Instagram rules.** Automated DMs must follow Meta's messaging policies (including the 24-hour reply window).
- **People.** If the coach desk depends on Lucas alone it stalls when he is away. Write a playbook and train a second person.
- **Price acceptance.** J&W's customers are value-minded. The free coach and à la carte option stay; review plan prices after 3 months.

### Planner logic for the builder
- Targets: Mifflin-St Jeor BMR (men 10·kg + 6.25·cm − 5·age + 5; women −161) × activity (0–1 days 1.2; 2–3 1.375; 4–5 1.55; 6–7 1.725) × goal (lose 0.80, maintain 1.00, build 1.10, GLP-1 0.75). Protein g/kg: lose 1.8, maintain 1.6, build 2.0, GLP-1 1.4 (cap 220 g). Fat 25% of kcal. Carbs = remainder ÷ 4. Round kcal to 10 and grams to 5.
- Full Day: breakfast is fixed (Egg White Bites + Steel Cut Oats: 290 kcal, P 16, C 31, F 9.5 est.); lunch and dinner each get (daily − breakfast) ÷ 2. Lunch & Dinner plan: each meal gets 35% of the day.
- Each meal = protein + carb + veg template. Veg fixed 110 g (140 g in GLP-1 mode). Carb grams = (meal carbs − veg carbs) ÷ carb per gram, clamped 60–200 g. Protein grams = (meal protein − carb and veg protein) ÷ protein per gram, clamped 85–255 g (GLP-1 cap 140 g). Round to 5 g. Macros use `perOz` ÷ 28.35 per gram. Exact-gram price = per-ounce price of the largest variant × grams ÷ 28.35. À la carte cart uses the variant nearest in grams, qty 1.
- Default Maria week (templates): Mon Souvlaki/White Basmati/Broccoli + Salmon/Quinoa/Asparagus · Tue Greek Lemon Chicken/Greek Lemon Potatoes/Green Beans + Roasted Turkey/Sweet Potato/Spinach · Wed Teriyaki/Cilantro Lime Rice/Broccoli & Cauliflower + Haddock/Brown Basmati/Brussels Sprouts · Thu Korean BBQ/White Basmati/Green Beans + Flank Steak/Baked Potato/Asparagus · Fri Arabic 7 Spice/Caribbean Rice & Beans/Mixed Veggies + Jerk Chicken/Sweet Potato/Green Beans. Standard-size cart total with 5 breakfasts: $159.12; exact grams $186.95.
- Size: Regular ≤120 g protein a day, Plus 121–160 g, Performance >160 g.
