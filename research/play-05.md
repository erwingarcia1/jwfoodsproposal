# Play 5: Corporate Gift Studio

Prepared by Minastech for J&W Foods / J&W Meal Butler. Date: October 16, 2026. All money in CAD. Every figure is an estimate unless it is J&W's own published price or a cited source.

**One line:** a self-serve gift studio where GTA employers, realtors, brokers and clinics send J&W boxes with their logo to a whole list, or send a Pick-a-Box certificate that each person redeems on J&W's own delivery routes.

**Why it makes money:** corporate gifting sells J&W's existing $85 to $200 boxes 20 at a time at about 40% gross margin. It brings in holiday cash and puts J&W in front of hundreds of new households each season.

**Modelled on:** [Omaha Steaks Business Gifts](https://www.omahasteaks.com/business-gifts) (global, Omaha, Nebraska). We borrow ideas only, never their copy.

## 1. What we borrow from Omaha Steaks, and what J&W does better

Omaha Steaks is a family butcher (founded 1917) with a dedicated business-gifting arm. It claims 5,000+ business clients, 350,000+ custom gift certificates and 700,000+ coolers delivered to business recipients since 2020. These are company claims from "Omaha Steaks internal data in 2025", verified on the live page on Oct 16, 2026 (competitor-05.md §2).

| Omaha idea (verified) | J&W version |
|---|---|
| "Choice" certificates: each recipient picks from a curated level and redeems online, by phone or mail | **J&W Pick-a-Box certificate** (our own name). Four levels. The recipient picks a box and books their own route day, or picks up free at Brimley Rd |
| Lead form with two one-tap qualifiers (gift count, spend per gift) | **"Your price in two taps"** right in the hero. It shows a real price at once, not a sales call |
| Multi-recipient ordering on an emailed Excel form, one invoice | **In-browser list upload.** It sorts every address onto J&W's real route days live, flags anyone outside Ontario, and turns missing addresses into certificates |
| Pay-on-redemption for large programs | Same, for 100+ certificate programs on approved accounts |
| "Customize Your Box" points builder with a sticky footer | A sticky studio bar (gifts · box · total · Continue) |
| Business Rewards by annual spend (US$5K / $20K / $50K) | **Gift Desk Rewards** at $2,500 / $7,500 / $15,000 a year, sized for GTA small and mid-size firms |
| Holiday planning calendar | **"Plan the year once"**: a year-round occasion calendar with reminders |
| Dark photo hero, "at a glance" card, big-number proof band, tier cards, FAQ, sticky action row | Same structure, in J&W navy with Mediterranean olive and terracotta |

**Where J&W wins:**
- **Omaha does not ship to Canada** ([Shipping](https://www.omahasteaks.com/info/Shipping-Information)). No Omaha-scale "let them choose" meat gift with local delivery exists in the GTA.
- J&W already runs its own weekly refrigerated routes. They go to Durham on Tuesday, Toronto and Mississauga on Wednesday, Halton and Hamilton on Thursday, and York and Peel on Friday, plus long routes to Ottawa, London and Windsor. Fees are $20 to $35, and pickup is free (jw-facts.md).
- It has its own Ontario-inspected plant (since 2008), and a family butcher story in Toronto that goes back to the early 1960s.
- **Clean Meal Butler meals as a gift.** The meals are salt and sugar free, with no added oils, which makes a wellness gift Omaha cannot offer.
- A Greek-Canadian identity (A Taste of Greece Box, 154 Mediterranean SKUs) suits Greek Easter gifting.
- Omaha's B2B flow is not self-serve. J&W's studio would be ahead of it on ease of use.

## 2. The mechanism (what J&W changes)

1. **Four gift levels built on boxes J&W already sells.** Every level stays under the CRA's $500 yearly non-cash gift limit ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/payroll/benefits-allowances/gifts-awards-social-events/gifts-awards-long-service-awards.html)).

   | Level | Real J&W boxes (price) | Concept additions |
   |---|---|---|
   | **Thank You · $90** | Ultimate BBQ Dad Pack $84.99 (AAA T-bone, AAA tenderloin, teriyaki salmon, 2 kg Korean BBQ chicken tenders). Girls Night Box $89.99 (chicken, pork and shrimp souvlaki, 4 top sirloin steaks, spanakopita, party bites) | Meal Butler Taster $87.93 |
   | **Well Done · $130** | Gentleman's Box $129.99 (2 AAA ribeyes 9-10 oz, 2 brisket bacon, hot Italian sausage, lean ground beef, 6 lamb chops). A Taste of Greece Box $130 (gyro, four souvlaki, Greek and Macedonian sausage, pita, tzatziki, 32 mini spanakopita). Steak Mania Combo $120 (8 steaks) | Meal Butler Clean Week $130 |
   | **Big Thanks · $200** | Long Weekend Box $199 (8 × 8 oz AAA striploins, back ribs, sausages, burgers, sliders, buns, fries) | Greek Easter Table $199 (Mar 1 to May 1, 2027) |
   | **Reserve · $350** | none | J&W Reserve Box $349 |

2. **Three ways to deliver:**
   - **One office drop** on the office's route day, for one route fee.
   - **Home delivery to each person** on their own route day, at J&W's usual fee per address ($20 GTA, $27 Halton/Hamilton, $35 long routes).
   - **A Pick-a-Box certificate** by email or printed card. Delivery is included for $20 a certificate, or the recipient picks up free at Brimley Rd. J&W waives the $100 route minimum for gift deliveries and redemptions.
3. **Branding:** the buyer's logo and message go on a printed card in every box, or on the certificate. This is free on every order, and the buyer gets a proof before printing.
4. **Volume pricing (Concept):** 10 to 49 gifts save 5%, 50 to 99 save 8%, 100 to 249 save 10%, and 250+ is quoted. The discount applies to the gifts only, never to delivery.
5. **Payment:** card at checkout, or invoice Net 30 for approved businesses (first order: 50% deposit). Large certificate programs (100+) can pay only for certificates that get redeemed.
6. **Year-round:**
   - An occasion calendar covers Lunar New Year (Feb 6, 2027), Employee Appreciation Day (Mar 5), Easter (Mar 28), Administrative Professionals Day (Apr 21), Greek Easter (May 2), Victoria Day (May 24), Father's Day (Jun 20), Canada Day, Labour Day and Thanksgiving (Oct 11, 2027).
   - Always-on gifts: realtor closings, work anniversaries, safety milestones and welcome-aboard boxes.
   - Reminders go out three weeks ahead with last year's list ready.
7. **Gift Desk Rewards (Concept):**
   - **Partner, $2,500+ a year:** priority December route slots, and the list is saved for next year.
   - **Preferred, $7,500+:** Partner perks, plus a J&W Reserve Box for the buyer ($349 value) and one named contact.
   - **Patron, $15,000+:** Preferred perks, plus pay-on-redemption certificates and a team tasting night at Brimley Rd.
8. **New customers:** each Pick-a-Box redemption asks for an email, with a CASL consent box left unchecked. Each box carries a "first order" card for the recipient.

### Fixes J&W must make before selling to businesses
- The A Taste of Greece Box description starts with pasted voice-dictation text ("Do you know what I know who it is…"). It also has an outdated "only until Thursday" line.
- Gendered box names (Gentleman's, Girls Night, Dad Pack) suit consumer gifting. The studio shows them under neutral level names, with the real product name underneath.
- Gift cards today are e-cards at $10 / $25 / $50 / $100 only, with no branding or bulk issue.

### Sanity checks
- Year 1 is about 5% of J&W's ~$2.5M mid-case revenue. The run-rate is about 14%.
- The upside case in market-benchmarks.md is 115 firms × 60 boxes × $120 ≈ $830K, which is 1% of the ~11–14K GTA locations with 50+ staff. Our year 2 uses about 70 accounts at 24 gifts, well inside that.
- The $120 average gift sits inside the $50–150 per-box estimate (market-benchmarks.md §6a) and Omaha's $100–$250 lead-form buckets.
- **Capacity:**
  - Holiday 2026 is about 500 gifts over 5 weeks, roughly 100 a week, with a peak near 280 in the week of Dec 14.
  - Holiday 2027 is about 1,700 gifts over 7 weeks, around 250 a week. It needs one seasonal packer and an extra Wednesday van run in December.
  - Certificates move part of the production into January.

### Notes on the math
- The 40% margin is the model's assumption for premium gift boxes. The $120 average already nets out volume pricing. On a box that earns 40% at list, an 8% discount brings margin down to about 35%, so big orders earn less per box.
- No certificate breakage is counted as revenue. Prepaid certificates carry no expiry date. Pay-on-redemption programs bill only redeemed codes.
- Revenue from other plays is not counted here:
  - No office meal programs (Play 4) and no wholesale (Play 2).
  - No Butcher Club subscriptions (Play 3) or fundraisers (Play 7).
  - If a later version lets certificates redeem for a Butcher Club month, the certificate value counts here once, and any later months count in Play 3.
- Follow-on orders are one-time orders only, so they are not double counted with subscriptions.

## 6. Assumptions
- The lean Holiday version is live by Mon Nov 9, 2026, and the full self-serve studio by Feb 1, 2027.
- Holiday 2026 is a partial season (25 accounts × 20 gifts). The buyers are mostly existing J&W customers who run businesses, Instagram followers (30K), and businesses reached by outreach along the routes.
- The average gift is $120 (mix of $85 to $350 after volume pricing).
- Gross margin is 40% (model assumption for gift boxes). The premium extras are listed under costs.
- Incrementality is 0.85.
- 60% of recipients are new to J&W, and 8% of those order once ($130) within the year.
- No breakage revenue is counted.
- 70% of Holiday 2026 accounts come back in year 2.
- J&W's real route fees apply, and the $100 route minimum is waived for gift deliveries and redemptions.
- Recipients must be in Ontario, because the plant is provincially inspected.

## 7. Risks
- **A late start for Holiday 2026.** Many firms choose gifts in October. Season 1 will be small. Certificates (no lead time) and existing customers are the fastest wins.
- **December capacity.** Gift volume collides with J&W's own holiday retail. Cap gifts per week (about 150 in Dec 2026) and use certificates to move redemptions into January. Watch the Meal Butler January peak.
- **Ontario-only sales.** National employers can't include staff outside Ontario. The studio must screen addresses before payment.
- **Cold chain at office drops.** Require a named receiver, a delivery window and a freezer or cooler nearby. Use insulated liners.
- **Credit risk on invoices.** Net 30 only for approved accounts, with a 50% deposit on first orders.
- **Gift card and tax rules.** J&W confirms certificate terms (no expiry on prepaid codes) with its accountant or lawyer. Buyers own their CRA treatment. J&W gives no tax advice, only a gift record.
- **Product copy.** The Taste of Greece description bug and the gendered names must be fixed first.
- **Seasonality.** About half of the run-rate lands in Nov–Dec.
- **Reputation.** Corporate buyers read reviews. Put the 4.8★ Google rating (611 reviews) on the page, and keep a professional Gift Desk face (jw-audit.md §2).

## 8. Prototype spec (one self-contained page, shared framework)

**File:** `site/prototypes/05-corporate-gift-studio.html` · `JWC.init({ n: 5, ... })`

**Concept:** this is a premium, self-serve corporate gifting page for J&W.
- A buyer gets a price in two taps, picks a level and a real J&W box, and adds a logo and message with a live card preview.
- They upload a recipient list, and it sorts itself onto J&W's real route days. It flags anyone outside Ontario and turns missing addresses into Pick-a-Box certificates.
- They pay by demo card through `JWC.checkout` or request an invoice. They can also see the page each recipient uses to redeem.
- The JW team view shows the Gift Desk pipeline, AI drafts waiting for approval, a certificate ledger, and the December cut list and route impact.

**The wow moment:** the buyer taps "Use our sample list (25 people)", and the list visibly sorts itself into route-day groups:
- Tue Durham 4 · Wed Toronto/Mississauga 7 · Wed long 1 · Thu Halton/Hamilton 3 · Fri York/Peel 6 · Fri long 1
- Calgary and Montréal are flagged: "We can only sell meat in Ontario. Removed, you won't be charged."
- One person with no address becomes a Pick-a-Box certificate.

At the same time, the branded card preview shows the buyer's logo and message, and the sticky bar updates the total.
