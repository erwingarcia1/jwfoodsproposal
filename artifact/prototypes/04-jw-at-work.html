For shift sites

The At Work Freezer.

For sites with 75 or more people on site, we lend you a glass-door freezer and keep it stocked. Good for night shifts, early starts and the days someone forgot to order.

  • Free loan. We deliver it, plug it in and restock it on your route day.
  • Staff scan the QR code, pick a meal and pay with their allowance or a card.
  • We count stock at every visit. If unpaid items go over 3% of what we stocked, the company covers the difference at work prices.
  • Frozen meals keep for months, so nothing is thrown out on Friday.

The site provides a standard outlet. The freezer loan terms are a Concept for discussion.

Before you ask

Fair questions.

The four things office managers ask us first.

“We don't have a kitchen.”

You need a microwave and some freezer space. Each bag shows its own heating steps. Sites with 75+ staff can borrow our freezer.

“Half the team is remote on Fridays.”

Meals are frozen, so they keep. Staff can take theirs home, and anyone can skip a week. No orders means no delivery and no bill.

“Healthy lunches are boring.”

Jerk chicken, Korean BBQ flank, Greek lemon souvlaki, teriyaki, honey garlic salmon. Our own marinades, cooked in our own plant.

“HR doesn't need another job.”

Staff order for themselves. You get one invoice and one short report a month. Reminders and order roll-ups are handled for you.
