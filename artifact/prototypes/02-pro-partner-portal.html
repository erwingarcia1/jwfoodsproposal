FAQ

Trade questions, plain answers

What’s the minimum order?

12 cooked meals or one butcher case. Standing orders of $150 or more drop free on your route day.

When do you deliver?

On our weekly routes: Tuesday Durham, Wednesday Toronto and Mississauga, Thursday Halton and Hamilton, Friday York and Peel. Order by Sunday for the next week. Pickup at Brimley Rd is always free.

How do I pay?

By card for your first four orders. After that, approved partners get net-15 invoices.

How does the freezer loan work?

Average $300 a week for eight weeks and we lend you a branded reach-in freezer with a temperature sensor. We own it and service it, and we take it back if you stop ordering. You supply the outlet.

How long does the food keep?

Everything is flash-frozen and vacuum-sealed, and each pack carries a best-before date. Keep it at −18°C or colder.

Can I sell online?

In person only. Your members can order from us online with your member code, and you earn credit.

Do labels show allergens and nutrition?

Every partner pack carries ingredients, allergens, net weight, best-before and our plant details. Nutrition facts are being added for partner products.

Can you deliver outside Ontario?

Not yet. Our plant is provincially inspected, so we sell within Ontario.
