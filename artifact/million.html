    How the grades work

    Each idea is scored on four tests that add up to 100, the same way for every idea. 80 and up reads “Strongest”, 70s “Strong”, 60s “Worth a test”, 50s “Later” and below 50 “Not now”. A lower score is not a no: the order on the path comes from timing (gifts sell in Q4, wellness budgets reset in January).

    Every idea, best first

    “Sales” are what customers would pay in a year, on top of what J&W sells today, once the idea is running. They are estimates and they are not profit: “you keep” is our estimate of gross profit after food, packaging, delivery and fees, before set-up costs, overheads and tax. Do not add them all up: several share the same customers, the same plant hours and the same team.
